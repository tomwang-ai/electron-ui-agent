# Code of Conduct

## English

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations. Reports should use the repository's private moderation or security channel when public discussion could expose personal information.

---

## 中文

请保持尊重、建设性并聚焦工作。骚扰、歧视、威胁或公开他人隐私信息的行为均不可接受。

项目维护者可以移除违反这些要求的内容或参与者。公开讨论可能暴露个人信息时，应通过仓库的私有管理或安全渠道报告。
